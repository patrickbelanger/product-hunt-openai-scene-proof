# Product Hunt launch

Name: SceneProof. Tagline: Your AI continuity supervisor for generative film.
Primary copy: Keep every shot in character. Alternative: Make your film remember itself.

Draft positioning: Generative films forget. Give SceneProof your visual bible and
footage. It compares your shots and references, explains continuity concerns with
visual evidence and gives you a correction prompt. When a change is intentional,
tell SceneProof and it adapts. Publish claims only after the corresponding flow works.

## 60–75 second recording

0–8s: show drift across generated shots. 8–15s: introduce SceneProof.
15–30s: demo film, references and real processing stages. 30–48s: wardrobe finding,
side-by-side evidence and correction. 48–65s: intentional-change explanation and
real re-evaluation. 65–75s: final product line and URL.
